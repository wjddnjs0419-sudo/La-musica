## Common Arrangement Flow

- Immediate rhythmic hook or vocal/percussion tag
- Drum groove appears quickly
- Main section repeats a short, aggressive rhythmic idea
- Breaks are short and often used for tension or call-and-response
- Energy stays raw and loop-driven

## Rhythm / Drums

- Tamborzao-inspired pulse
- Fast syncopated kick/clap hits
- Percussion fills create forward chaos
- Whistle or FX accents can mark hooks
- Repetition is short, direct, and physical

## Bass

- Heavy distorted 808 pulses
- Bass often hits in short, aggressive bursts
- Low-end is loud and club-forward

## Instruments / Texture

- Baile percussion
- Whistle accents
- Minimal synth stabs
- Raw vocal spaces or chant-like gaps
- Gritty loudness

## Energy Curve

- Immediate impact
- Loop-based intensity
- Short breaks
- Repeated hook pressure

## Prompt-safe Descriptor

`tamborzao-inspired rhythmic pattern, fast syncopated kick and clap hits, baile percussion fills, heavy distorted 808 pulses, whistle accents, short call-and-response hook spaces, raw loop-driven party energy, polished but gritty club loudness`
