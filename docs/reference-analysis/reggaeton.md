## Reference Pool Notes

The public playlist metadata points toward Latin urban, club reggaeton, dembow-driven rhythm, and modern pop-reggaeton crossover.

This analysis should not copy any artist flow, melody, or hook. Use only common reggaeton production grammar.

## Common Arrangement Flow

- Intro starts with plucked motif, vocal texture, or filtered dembow percussion
- Dembow groove enters early and remains the rhythmic identity
- Verse keeps space for vocal rhythm while bass and percussion stay steady
- Hook repeats a short melodic/rhythmic phrase over fuller drums
- Break or bridge strips percussion briefly before final hook
- Final hook often adds extra shakers, ad-libs, and wider mix density

## Rhythm / Drums

- Dembow pocket as the core pattern
- Kick emphasizes downbeats and groove anchors
- Snare/clap accents sit between beats to create syncopation
- Shakers, rim clicks, and small percussion create rolling motion
- Drum pattern is repetitive but has small fills around transitions

## Bass

- Rolling sub or 808 follows the dembow pocket
- Bass is often short, rounded, and club-weighted
- Occasional slide or accent into hook sections

## Instruments / Texture

- Nylon guitar or plucked synth motif
- Warm tropical chord stabs
- Dark or glossy synth pads for late-night mood
- Sparse melodic loop that leaves space for vocal

## Energy Curve

- Groove-first rather than build/drop-first
- Hook increases density more than harmonic complexity
- Percussion layering controls energy

## Prompt-safe Descriptor

`tight dembow groove, kick anchoring the downbeats, snare-clap accents between beats, syncopated shaker and rim percussion, rolling sub and 808 bass following the pocket, short plucked synth or nylon-guitar motif, warm tropical chord stabs, glossy modern Latin urban mix`
