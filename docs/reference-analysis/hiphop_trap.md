## Reference Pool Notes

The public playlist metadata points toward current rap/trap anthems, heavy bass, modern flows, and sparse but hard-hitting production.

Do not imitate any rapper's flow, ad-libs, lyrical content, or exact beat.

## Common Arrangement Flow

- Intro often starts with a sparse melodic loop, riser, tag-like space, or filtered drums
- Main beat drops quickly with 808 and snare establishing pocket
- Verse leaves open space for rhythmic phrasing
- Hook increases ad-libs, bass movement, or melodic repetition
- Beat may drop out briefly before hook returns
- Outro often loops the main motif or strips back drums

## Rhythm / Drums

- Half-time feel
- Snare or clap commonly anchors beat three
- 16th-note hi-hat rolls with triplet bursts
- Percussive fills and hat stutters mark transitions
- Kick pattern syncopates around 808 accents

## Bass

- 808 is a lead instrument, not just support
- Slides, glides, and pitch bends create movement
- Sub-heavy low end with short decay or distorted sustain depending mood

## Instruments / Texture

- Sparse minor piano, bell, flute, synth pluck, or guitar loop
- Atmospheric pads or reversed textures
- Minimal harmonic movement
- Dark negative space

## Energy Curve

- Energy comes from pocket, bass, and vocal rhythm
- Avoid overfilling the arrangement
- Hook can be denser but should preserve low-end clarity

## Prompt-safe Descriptor

`half-time trap drum pocket, punchy snare on beat three, crisp 16th-note hi-hat rolls with triplet fills, syncopated kick pattern, booming 808 slides and glides, sparse minor-key piano or bell motif, atmospheric pads, open space for rhythmic rap phrasing, clean low-end heavy mix`

## Suggested Preset Delta

Current preset is strong. Consider emphasizing `minimal harmonic movement` and `negative space` if generations become too busy.
