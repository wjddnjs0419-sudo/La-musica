## Common Arrangement Flow

- Short intro with filtered drums, vocal chop, synth riff, or riser
- Beat establishes four-on-the-floor pulse quickly
- Build-up uses snare rolls, rising noise, pitch risers, and tension automation
- Drop is the main energy payoff with full kick, bass, chord stabs, and wider stereo image
- Breakdown removes kick and lowers density
- Final drop returns bigger, brighter, or more layered

## Rhythm / Drums

- Four-on-the-floor kick as the anchor
- Offbeat open hats or shuffled top-loop motion
- Clap/snare accents around the backbeat
- Snare rolls into drops
- Short fills before section changes

## Bass

- Sidechained sub or electro bass pumping against the kick
- Root-note bass movement with strong low-end consistency
- Drop section often uses wider, more aggressive bass layers

## Instruments / Texture

- Supersaw chords
- Bright synth stabs
- Vocal chops or short melodic hooks
- Noise sweeps, risers, downlifters
- Wide stereo pads in breakdowns

## Energy Curve

- Fast setup
- Tension build
- Large drop payoff
- Short relief
- Second bigger payoff

## Prompt-safe Descriptor

`four-on-the-floor dance rhythm around 126-132 BPM, punchy sidechained kick, offbeat open hats, snare-roll build-ups, rising noise sweeps, layered supersaw chord stabs, wide sub bass, bright festival drop dynamics, clean high-energy electronic mix`
