## Reference Pool Notes

The public playlist metadata points toward hard techno, rave, melodic techno, industrial texture, and peak-time club energy.

Do not copy any specific synth riff or drop.

## Common Arrangement Flow

- DJ-friendly intro with kick or filtered percussion
- Bassline or synth sequence enters gradually
- Hypnotic loop evolves through filter automation
- Breakdowns remove kick and build tension with risers/noise
- Drop reintroduces kick and bass with increased intensity
- Long sections rely on subtle automation rather than pop-style hooks

## Rhythm / Drums

- Relentless four-on-the-floor kick
- Closed-hat pulse and offbeat hats
- Metallic percussion loops
- Snare/clap used sparingly or as tension accent
- Repetition is intentional and hypnotic

## Bass

- Rolling 16th-note bassline or pulsing low-end
- Bass locks tightly to kick
- Low-end is controlled, dark, and club-focused

## Instruments / Texture

- Minor synth sequences
- Acid or resonant filter motion when appropriate
- Industrial hits
- Noise sweeps
- Warehouse reverb

## Energy Curve

- Slow-burn tension
- Hypnotic repetition
- Peak-time drop through reintroduction of kick/bass
- Less melodic hook, more texture and pressure

## Prompt-safe Descriptor

`relentless four-on-the-floor kick, rolling 16th-note bassline, tight closed-hat pulse, metallic percussion loops, hypnotic minor synth sequence, gradual filter automation, industrial noise accents, breakdown tension, dark warehouse reverb, peak-time club pressure`
