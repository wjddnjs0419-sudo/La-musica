## Reference Pool Notes

The public playlist metadata points toward Afrobeats/Afropop, West African pop, party energy, warm melodic grooves, and festival-friendly dance music.

Do not copy a specific artist's topline, lyrics, drum loop, or vocal style.

## Common Arrangement Flow

- Intro with guitar riff, percussion, or soft vocal texture
- Groove enters with syncopated drums and buoyant bass
- Verse stays light and melodic
- Chorus/hook opens with brighter guitars, group-like response, or melodic repetition
- Bridge or break reduces percussion before final hook
- Final chorus adds percussion layers and wider ambience

## Rhythm / Drums

- Syncopated kick pattern
- Shuffling hats
- Hand percussion and djembe-like layers
- Groove feels buoyant rather than heavy
- Percussion density controls energy

## Bass

- Warm buoyant bassline
- Bass moves melodically with the groove
- Low-end supports dancing without overpowering percussion

## Instruments / Texture

- Bright clean guitar riffs
- Warm brass stabs
- Hand percussion
- Sunny pads or soft synths
- Spacious outdoor-stage ambience

## Energy Curve

- Sunny, open, and danceable
- Chorus lifts through brightness and percussion density
- Final hook feels communal and festival-sized
