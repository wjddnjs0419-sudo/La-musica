## Reference Pool Notes

The public references point toward call-and-response hooks, simple repeated phrases, stomp-clap pulse, brass/rock/dance crossover, and crowd identity.

Do not copy any club anthem lyrics, melody, or team-specific chant.

## Common Arrangement Flow

- Intro with stomp, clap, drum hit, whistle, or brass call
- Crowd hook enters early
- Verse/chant section uses short repeated phrases
- Chorus is simple, loud, and easy to repeat
- Break leaves space for crowd response
- Final chorus widens with bigger drums, brass, and crowd reverb

## Rhythm / Drums

- Stomp-clap pulse
- Big floor toms
- Snare hits
- Simple march-like rhythm
- Claps emphasize collective participation

## Bass

- Heavy supportive bass
- Bass reinforces chant rhythm rather than complex melody
- Low-end should feel stadium-sized

## Instruments / Texture

- Brass stabs
- Whistle accents
- Crowd shouts
- Big reverb
- Simple repeated hook

## Energy Curve

- Immediate communal identity
- Short phrases
- Repeatable hook
- Final section should feel like a stadium singing together

## Prompt-safe Descriptor

`stadium chant rhythm, stomp-clap pulse, big floor toms and snare hits, brass stabs, whistle accents, simple call-and-response hook shape, short repeatable crowd phrases, heavy supportive bass, wide crowd-sized reverb, explosive sports celebration chorus`
