## Reference Pool Notes

The public playlist metadata points toward emotional Korean ballads, OST-style songs, piano/string arrangements, and karaoke-friendly vocal arcs.

Do not copy melodies, lyrics, toplines, or a specific singer's tone.

## Common Arrangement Flow

- Intro with solo piano, guitar, or soft pad
- Verse begins restrained with sparse accompaniment
- Pre-chorus lifts harmony and adds strings or percussion
- Chorus widens with strings, drums, and emotional vocal peak
- Verse 2 adds subtle rhythmic support
- Bridge often strips down or modulates emotional tension
- Final chorus is the biggest section with full drums/strings

## Rhythm / Drums

- Slow-to-mid tempo
- Verse drums are minimal or absent
- Chorus may add soft drum kit or restrained ballad percussion
- Final chorus may use a gradual drum build

## Bass

- Warm supportive bass
- Root-note movement supports emotional chord progression
- Bass should not dominate the vocal

## Instruments / Texture

- Warm piano arpeggios
- Acoustic guitar support
- Lyrical strings
- Soft pads
- Gentle cymbal swells
- Karaoke-ready polish

## Energy Curve

- Starts intimate
- Builds emotional pressure
- Chorus opens wide
- Final chorus delivers the largest emotional lift

## Prompt-safe Descriptor

`slow-to-mid tempo Korean ballad arrangement, intimate piano or acoustic guitar intro, restrained verse dynamics, warm supportive bass, lyrical string orchestra swells, emotional pre-chorus lift, wide chorus payoff, gradual drum build into the final chorus, polished karaoke-ready mix`
